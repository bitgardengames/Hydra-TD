Core Campaign Philosophy
Hydra TD should use this structure:

The player should win because they learned placement, tower roles, upgrades, targeting, and abilities.

Power should come from in-map economy and upgrades, not meta-level damage boosts.

Every tower remains relevant all campaign.

Lancer stays the efficient baseline.

Slow stays the control multiplier.

Cannon handles packs and armor.

Poison handles sustained damage and regeneration.

Shock handles chained groups.

Plasma handles expensive zone/priority damage.

Keep the game legible.

Simple map order.

Simple star/medal progression.

Simple tower role descriptions.

Simple enemy introductions.

Simple difficulty differences.
